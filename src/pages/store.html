Questions

Buying from the Mizani Store

How do I pay?

Check out with your details and you will get an order number. Pay by M-Pesa Paybill 880100 (account 1011645723) or by bank transfer to NCBA Bank, account PEMS MIZANI LIMITED. Then send us your payment confirmation with your order number.

How much is shipping?

We ship worldwide. Shipping depends on where you are, so we confirm the cost with you after you order and before we dispatch.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Every item is handmade, so colours and patterns vary slightly from piece to piece. That is part of their character.

Can I order a custom piece or buy wholesale?

Yes. Ask us on WhatsApp or through the enquiry form below with what you have in mind.

Where does the money go?

Proceeds fund Mizani's programs for young people in Kenya: curriculum materials, sanitary pads, mentorship camps and educator training.
