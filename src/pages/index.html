Who we are

Mizani is Swahili for balance. Balance is our life's work.

We believe a young person who is physically strong, emotionally grounded, socially connected and mentally clear has what they need to change their world.

"We are not trying to fix young people. We are creating the conditions for them to become fully themselves."
