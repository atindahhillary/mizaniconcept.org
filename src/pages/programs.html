Flagship · Sekenani Youth Camp

Mentorship camps for teens

Held in Sekenani, Narok County, near the Maasai Mara, our immersive multi-day camps bring teens and pre-teens together for structured mentorship, peer connection, physical wellness and deep engagement with all 4Cs. Camps are where transformation becomes visible, and where young people who are often overlooked discover they have a place and a purpose.

Measurement

How we know our work is working

To track the wellbeing and progress of the young people in our programs, Mizani uses the Miracle Foundation's Thrive Scale™, a five-domain assessment.

  • Education
  • Physical and mental health
  • Family and social relationships
  • Home finances
  • Living conditions

The Thrive Scale™ is not our core business. Our core business is developing whole people. It is our evidence layer: a tested tool that shows us what is working and where to go deeper.
