Questions

Giving, answered

How do I give from Kenya?

Open M-Pesa, choose Lipa na M-Pesa, then Pay Bill. Enter business number 880100 and account number 1011645723, then your amount and PIN. The payment goes to Mizani's NCBA account, PEMS MIZANI LIMITED.

Can I give from outside Kenya?

Yes. Send a bank transfer to NCBA Bank, Kahawa Sukari Branch, account name PEMS MIZANI LIMITED, account number 1011645723, SWIFT/BIC CBAFKENX. Use "Donation" and your name as the reference.

Will I get a receipt?

Yes. Send your M-Pesa or bank confirmation to us on WhatsApp (+254 724 116694) or email (info@mizaniconcept.org) and we will send a thank-you receipt.

What exactly does $10 cover?

One student's mentorship, wellness sessions and curriculum materials for a full school term.

Can my company or school partner with Mizani?

We welcome partners who bring tools and teaching aids, sponsor camp places or help build safe spaces. Tell us about your idea.

Can I see the work before I give?

Yes. Donors are welcome to visit the field or attend a camp. Arrange a visit.
